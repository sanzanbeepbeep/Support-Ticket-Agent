## Refund policy
- Duplicate or erroneous charges are always refunded in full. Only the **billing team** can issue refunds or void
  authorizations.
- Refunds appear on the customer's statement within 5-10 business days depending on the bank.

## When the customer threatens a chargeback or bank dispute
A chargeback costs a dispute fee and can lock the account. Treat it as urgent:
1. Escalate to billing immediately and mark the ticket as at risk of a chargeback.
2. Reply quickly with empathy. Confirm that the charges are being reviewed by a person and that the customer
   will receive written confirmation once they are voided or refunded.
3. Do not argue about the dispute and do not guarantee a timeline that depends on the bank.
