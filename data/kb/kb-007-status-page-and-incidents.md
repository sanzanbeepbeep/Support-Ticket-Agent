---
id: KB-007
title: Status page shows operational but customers report problems
tags: status page, status, operational, incident, region, asia, europe, outage, regional
---
The public status page (status.company.com) is updated manually by the incident commander, usually 15-30 minutes
after an incident is declared. Regional incidents that affect only part of the infrastructure may not be shown
there at first.

Internal monitoring is the source of truth. When a customer says the status page is green but they are affected,
check internal service status for their region. Never tell a customer "everything is operational" based only on
the public status page.
