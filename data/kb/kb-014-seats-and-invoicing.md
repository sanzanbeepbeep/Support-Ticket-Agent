---
id: KB-014
title: Adding seats and invoice changes on Enterprise contracts
tags: seats, add seats, licenses, enterprise, invoice, currency, billing address, contract, renewal
---
- Enterprise seat changes are handled by the account's **Customer Success Manager** (account_management team)
  through a contract amendment. Added seats are prorated to the renewal date.
- Invoices can be issued in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, SGD or THB. Currency and billing-address changes apply from the
  next invoice and need confirmation from the billing contact on file.
- Pro and Free plans add seats themselves in Settings > Members.
