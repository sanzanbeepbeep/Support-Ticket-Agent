id: KB-012
title: Account deletion and personal data requests (GDPR / PDPA)
tags: delete account, deletion, remove data, personal data, gdpr, pdpa, privacy, close account
---
- Users can delete their own account in Settings > Account > Delete account. All projects and personal data are
  permanently deleted within **30 days**. Backups are purged within 90 days.
- If the user cannot log in, or asks for a copy of their data first, route the ticket to **account_management**,
  which verifies ownership before acting.
- Support must never delete an account on the user's behalf without identity verification.
- These requests are legal obligations under GDPR (EU) and PDPA (Thailand): reply within 3 business days.
