## Triage questions
- Does it affect one user or many? Does it happen on several browsers and devices?
- A single user on a single browser is usually local (cache, extensions): suggest clearing the cache or trying a
  private window.
- **Many users or several browsers** means the problem is server-side. Do not ask the customer to troubleshoot further.

## Server-side errors
1. Check internal service status for the customer's region.
2. If there is an active incident, or the account is Enterprise, escalate to **SRE** at once and link the incident.
3. Tell the customer the issue is confirmed and being worked on, and that they will get updates. Do not give an
   ETA unless the incident commander has published one.
