---
id: KB-011
title: Cancelling a subscription and refund eligibility
tags: cancel, cancellation, refund, money back, annual, monthly, subscription, downgrade
---
- **Annual plans** can be refunded in full within **14 days** of purchase. After 14 days the plan stays active
  until the end of the term and is not renewed.
- **Monthly plans** are not refunded. Cancelling stops the next renewal.
- Customers can cancel themselves in Settings > Billing > Cancel plan.
- Refunds are issued by the **billing team** after support confirms eligibility. Support must not promise the
  refund before billing has approved it.
