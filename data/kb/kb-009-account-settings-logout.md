title: Account settings, profile picture and logging out
tags: account, profile, picture, avatar, logout, log out, sign out, settings, email change
---
- **Log out**: click your avatar in the top-right corner > Sign out. On the desktop app use File > Sign out.
- **Profile picture**: avatar > Profile > Upload photo (JPG or PNG, max 5 MB).
- **Change email**: Profile > Email. A confirmation link is sent to the new address.
