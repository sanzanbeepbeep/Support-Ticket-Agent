title: Enterprise support SLA and escalation
tags: enterprise, sla, p1, priority, escalation, customer success manager, csm, business impact
---
- P1 (production down or most users blocked): first response within 1 hour, resolution target 4 hours, 24/7.
- P2 (major feature degraded): first response within 4 business hours.
- For every P1, notify the account's Customer Success Manager (CSM) and, if there is an active incident, link the
  ticket to it.
- Enterprise customers can ask for a written incident report (RCA) after resolution.
