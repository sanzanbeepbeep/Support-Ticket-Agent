title: Pro plan features and export
tags: pro, plan, features, export, pdf, pptx, presentation, upgrade

Pro unlocks:
- Export to PDF, PPTX and high-resolution PNG.
- Unlimited projects and version history.
- Priority email support.

Pro features become available immediately after a successful upgrade, or during a temporary Pro trial granted by
support. Users on the Free plan can export to PNG only, with a watermark.
